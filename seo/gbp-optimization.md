# Delta Construction — Google Business Profile Optimisation

Current profile audited from the live GBP. Ordered by impact. GBP is the single
biggest driver of local-pack visibility and phone calls for a builder, so this
matters more right now than more website pages.

---

## 🔴 CRITICAL FIXES (do first)

### 1. Business name — fix the redundant, messy name
**Current:** `Delta Construction Limited UK Ltd`  ← "Limited" + "Ltd" is redundant and looks spammy.
**Change to:** the exact registered/trading name, cleanly — e.g. **`Delta Construction`** (or `Delta Construction Ltd` if that's the Companies House name).
- Google reviews name edits (can take days) and **rejects keyword/location stuffing** — so NO "London builders" etc. in the name.
- ⚠️ This must be the **same name used everywhere** (website, directories, disavow/citations work). Pick one and lock it. Right now the site says "Delta Construction Ltd UK" and GBP says "…Limited UK Ltd" — inconsistent. Standardise.

### 2. Website URL — wrong protocol + wrong host
**Current:** `http://www.deltaconstructionltd.co.uk/`
**Change to:** `https://deltaconstructionltd.co.uk/`
- It's `http` (not secure) and `www` (which now 308-redirects to the apex). Point it at the real canonical URL so Google follows a clean, direct link.

### 3. Categories — you have ONE; add secondaries (huge, free win)
**Primary (keep):** Construction company
**Add secondary categories** (only the ones genuinely true — each unlocks new searches):
- General contractor
- Home builder
- Building restoration service
- Roofing contractor
- Masonry contractor / Bricklayer
- Kitchen remodeler
- Bathroom remodeler
- Landscaper
> Categories are one of the strongest local-ranking levers. A single category
> is leaving most of your relevant searches on the table.

### 4. Location & service areas — align with where he actually is/works
**Current:** No location set (service-area business) + service areas = Chelsea, Notting Hill, Hammersmith & Fulham, London.
**Problem:** He's based in **Hounslow** and the website targets Hounslow / West / SW London — but the GBP service areas point at prime-central areas far from base. Local ranking is strongest near the verified location, so this mismatch hurts.
**Fix:**
- Verify with the **real Hounslow base address** (you can keep it hidden — "I deliver goods and services to my customers"). This anchors the profile to Hounslow.
- Set **service areas to the real catchment**, matching the website area pages (up to ~20):
  `Hounslow, Isleworth, Brentford, Feltham, Heston, Richmond, Twickenham, Kingston, Ealing, Chiswick, Hammersmith, Acton, Chelsea, Kensington, Wimbledon, Putney, Wandsworth`

## 🟢 ONGOING (compounding trust)

### 8. Reviews — your strongest local-pack signal
- You have 16 five-stars. Set up a routine: every finished job → ask for a Google review, send the review link by text.
- Reply to **every** review (thank them, name the project/area — adds relevance).
- Steady, recent reviews matter more than a big one-off batch.

### 9. Google Posts — post every 1–2 weeks
- A finished project, an offer ("free site surveys in Hounslow"), a tip. Keeps the profile active (a ranking/engagement signal) and shows in the profile.

### 10. Q&A — seed it
- Post 3–5 common questions yourself and answer them ("Do you offer free quotes?", "What areas do you cover?", "Are you insured?"). Owner-answered Q&A improves conversion.

### 11. Attributes / "From the business"
- Fill in service options and any relevant attributes. Consider enabling **online estimates/quotes** (currently off) to capture leads.

### 12. Social profiles
- The pending Facebook + Instagram links are good — keep them; make sure NAP on those matches too.
