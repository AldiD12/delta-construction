## How to post (quick)
GBP → **Add update / Post** → pick type → add **photo** → text → **button** → link.

## Rules
- **Every post needs a photo** (real project shots are best). Min 720×540; 1200×900 ideal.
- First ~80 characters are what people see before "read more" — front-load the hook.
- One clear **button**: "Learn more" (to a page) or "Call now".
- Keep it real — no fake offers, no invented projects.
- "What's new" posts fade after ~7 days, so keep a steady drip.
- Use natural local wording (Hounslow, West London) — don't stuff.

## What to watch after posting
GBP → **Insights → Posts** shows views/clicks per post. After ~4 weeks you'll
see which post types actually drive clicks — double down on those. If the
"recently completed" ones are winning (they usually do), that's your cue to
photograph every finished job and post it.
