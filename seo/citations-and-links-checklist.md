# Delta Construction — Citations & Links Action Checklist

The goal: replace a toxic, spam-only backlink profile with a small number of
**real, trusted, local** citations and links. For a London builder these drive
the **Google local pack** (the map + 3 results) — which is where most phone
calls actually come from.

---

## STEP 0 — Lock your NAP (do this first, it underpins everything)

"NAP" = Name, Address, Phone. It must be **identical** everywhere online —
same spelling, same format, same phone. Inconsistent NAP is the #1 reason
local rankings stall. Decide the canonical version now and copy-paste it every
time:

```
Name:    Delta Construction Ltd
Address: [EXACT registered/trading address, Hounslow — fill in once, use everywhere]
Phone:   +44 7479 389 996
Email:   info@deltaconstructionltd.co.uk
Web:     https://deltaconstructionltd.co.uk
```

⚠️ Pick ONE phone number and ONE address format and never vary them.

## TIER 1 — The big three (free, do this week)

These are the highest-impact citations. GBP alone outweighs everything else for
a local trade.

- [ ] **Google Business Profile** — claim/verify, choose primary category
      "Construction company" (+ secondary: "Building restoration", "Home builder",
      "Roofing contractor" as relevant), set **service areas** (Hounslow, West
      London, Richmond, etc.), add **photos** (your real project pics), fill hours,
      services, and a description. → google.com/business
- [ ] **Bing Places for Business** — bingplaces.com (can import from Google)
- [ ] **Apple Business Connect** — businessconnect.apple.com (shows in Apple Maps)

## TIER 2 — UK trade directories (free tiers; consistent NAP)

Real citations + many send genuine leads. Free listings are enough to start.

- [ ] **Checkatrade** — high trust, strong for builders (paid, but lead-driving)
- [ ] **MyBuilder** — lead platform + citation
- [ ] **Rated People** — lead platform + citation
- [ ] **TrustATrader**
- [ ] **Houzz** — create a **Pro profile + project photos** (also an authority link)
- [ ] **Yell.com**

- [ ] **Cylex UK**
- [ ] **Scoot**
- [ ] **Thomson Local**
- [ ] **192.com**
- [ ] **Hotfrog UK**

> Tip: do 2–3 per week, not all at once — a sudden burst looks unnatural.

---

## TIER 3 — Trade accreditations (paid/vetted, highest trust + win customers)

These cost money and require vetting, but each gives a **strong authority link**
AND is a genuine trust signal homeowners look for. Worth more than 100 directory
listings.

- [ ] **Federation of Master Builders (FMB)** — the big one for UK builders
- [ ] **TrustMark** — government-endorsed quality scheme
- [ ] **Guild of Master Craftsmen**
- [ ] **Which? Trusted Traders**
- [ ] **CIOB** (Chartered Institute of Building) — if eligible
- [ ] Manufacturer **"approved/registered installer"** pages for the products you
      fit (windows, roofing systems, kitchens) — ask your suppliers

---

## TIER 4 — Local & earned links (the strongest, hardest to copy)

- [ ] **Local Chamber of Commerce** (Hounslow / West London) — membership = link
- [ ] **Supplier & merchant sites** — builders' merchants, kitchen/roofing suppliers
      often list trade customers; just ask
- [ ] **Architects & designers** you work with — reciprocal links / "our builders"
- [ ] **Local sponsorship** — a local football/cricket team, school fair, charity:
      sponsor pages almost always link back, and it's real community marketing
- [ ] **Local press** — a finished project, a milestone, a hire: pitch the local
      paper / community news site
- [ ] **Houzz / Instagram project features** — real photos, linked to site

## ❌ Never again

- No "buy X backlinks" gigs (Fiverr, Telegram, cold emails)
- No PBNs, "niche edits", "guest post packages", DR/DA/TF "boosters"
- Nothing promising fast rankings via links
- If anyone shows you fake testimonials like the ones in your Ahrefs export —
  walk away. That's the product that got you the toxic profile in the first place.

**Reviews note:** alongside links, actively ask happy customers for **Google
reviews**. Review quantity/recency is one of the strongest local-pack signals —
arguably more than links for a local builder. You have 18; aim for a steady drip.
