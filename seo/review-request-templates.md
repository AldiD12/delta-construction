# Delta Construction — Review Request Templates

Reviews are the strongest local-pack (map) ranking signal for a builder, and
past clients are the quickest win. Below: how to get the one-tap link, then
ready-to-send messages, plus reply templates.

---

## STEP 1 — Get the one-tap review link (do once)

Every message must contain a link that opens the review box in **one tap** — no
"search for us on Google" (people won't).

**To get it:** in the Google Business Profile → **"Ask for reviews"** / **"Get
more reviews"** → copy the short link. It looks like `https://g.page/r/xxxx/review`.
Paste that link into the `[REVIEW LINK]` slot below.

> Tip: shorten it and make it memorable with a free Bitly, e.g. `bit.ly/review-delta`,
> and put it in your email signature and invoices too.

---

## STEP 2 — The golden rules

- ✅ Ask when they're **happiest** — at handover, or right after they say "we love it".
- ✅ **Personalise** every message (their name + the actual project + area).
- ✅ Make it **one tap** (the link above).
- ✅ **Space them out** — a few a week, not 16 in one day (a sudden flood looks fake).
- ✅ **Reply to every review** (templates below) — replies are themselves a signal.
- ❌ **Never offer money, discounts or gifts** for reviews — it violates Google policy and can get reviews removed.
- ❌ Never buy reviews. (Same lesson as the backlinks — fake = harmful.)

## STEP 3 — Templates

### A. Text / SMS — just finished the job (short = best)
> Hi [Name], it's [Your name] at Delta Construction — really enjoyed working on your [loft conversion] in [Ealing]. If you're happy with how it turned out, a quick Google review would mean a lot and helps other local families find us: [REVIEW LINK] — thank you!

**Variant (warmer):**
> Hi [Name], thanks again for having us for your [kitchen extension]. It'd genuinely help us if you could leave a short Google review about how it went — takes 30 seconds here: [REVIEW LINK]. Either way, it was a pleasure. — [Your name], Delta Construction

### B. WhatsApp — after a photo/handover
> Hi [Name] 👋 So pleased with how your [rear extension] came out — thanks for trusting us with it. If you've got a spare minute, a quick Google review helps us more than you'd think: [REVIEW LINK] 🙏 Any snags at all, just message me first.

### C. Email — PAST clients (from months/years ago)
**Subject:** A quick favour, [Name]?

> Hi [Name],
>
> I hope you're still enjoying the [loft conversion] we completed at [address/area] — hard to believe it's been [X months]!
>
> We're a small, director-led team and word of mouth is everything for us. If you were happy with the work, would you consider leaving us a short Google review? It genuinely helps other homeowners in [area] find a builder they can trust.
>
> It takes less than a minute here: [REVIEW LINK]
>
> No pressure at all — and if there's ever anything you need looking at, just reply to this email.
>
> With thanks,
> [Your name]
> Delta Construction · 07479 389996

### D. In-person / handover script (say it out loud)
> "We're really proud of how this turned out. We're a small local firm, so honest Google reviews make a huge difference for us — would you mind leaving one? I'll text you the link now so it's right there on your phone."
> *(Then send Template A immediately while you're standing there.)*

### E. Gentle follow-up (only if no response after ~1 week — send ONCE)
> Hi [Name], no worries if you've been busy — just floating my earlier message in case it's handy. A quick Google review would really help us out: [REVIEW LINK]. Thanks either way! — [Your name]

## STEP 4 — Reply to every review (owner responses)

Replying adds keywords/area naturally and shows you're active. Keep it human.

### 5-star reply (vary these — don't copy-paste identically)
> Thank you, [Name] — it was a genuine pleasure building your [loft conversion] in [area]. Enjoy the new space, and don't hesitate to call if you ever need us again. — [Your name], Delta Construction

> Really appreciate this, [Name]. Projects like your [kitchen extension] are exactly why we love what we do. All the best from the Delta Construction team.

### 4-star / minor niggle
> Thanks for the kind words and the honest feedback, [Name]. We're always looking to improve — I'd love to hear what would have made it a 5. Either way, it was great working with you on your [project].

### Negative / unhappy review (stay calm, take it offline)
> Hi [Name], I'm sorry to hear this and I take it seriously. This isn't the standard we hold ourselves to. Could you call me directly on 07479 389996 so I can understand what happened and put it right? — [Your name], Delta Construction
>
> *(Never argue publicly. A calm, solution-focused reply reassures the next 100 readers more than the review itself worries them.)*

---

## Quick campaign plan for PAST clients
1. List every past client with a phone/email (aim for your best 15–25 jobs).
2. Send **3–4 per week** (Template C for old jobs, A/B for recent).
3. Log who you've asked so you don't double-ask.
4. Reply to each review as it lands.
5. Going forward: make Template A part of every project handover — automatic.
