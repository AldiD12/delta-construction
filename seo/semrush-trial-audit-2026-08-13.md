# Semrush trial audit — Delta Construction

Date: 13 August 2026  
Target: Hounslow, England, United Kingdom (desktop)

## Executive finding

The site is technically healthy enough to rank, but its organic footprint is still shallow. Semrush finds 123 organic keywords and an estimated 11 monthly visits, while 105 ranking keywords point to the homepage. The immediate opportunity is to build relevance and authority for the service pages rather than create more broad, generic content.

- Site Health: 90%
- AI Search Health: 89%
- Organic keywords: 123
- Estimated organic traffic: 11
- Estimated traffic value: $33
- Position Tracking: 70 keywords, 0.09% local visibility, average position 96.93
- Ranked tracked keywords: 4 of 70
- Authority Score: 6
- Backlinks: 95 from 60 referring domains (43 follow, 52 nofollow)

Known tracked positions include `construction company hounslow` at 33, `building contractors hounslow` at 48, `builders hounslow` at 49, and `construction company west london` at 55. Roofing terms are not yet in the top 100.

## Technical audit

Semrush crawled 33 of 100 allowed pages and reported 19 errors and 58 warnings.

### Fix first

1. Add the required `address` property to the LocalBusiness structured data. Semrush found this error on 19 pages.
2. Shorten the 13 overlong title tags, especially area, service and cost-guide pages.
3. Review the one sitemap orphan and strengthen internal links to important service pages.

The 29 “broken external links” are WhatsApp links returning HTTP 429 to the Semrush crawler. They are crawler rate-limit noise, not evidence that the customer-facing links are broken.

Low word-count flags on Contact and Gallery are not automatically defects. Improve pages only where additional content genuinely helps a visitor.

## On Page SEO Checker campaign

The automatic import was removed because it contained broad and irrelevant terms. A focused 20-keyword campaign was created for eight real landing pages: Roofing, Extensions, Loft Conversions, Kitchens & Bathrooms, Brickwork & Pointing, Hounslow, and the two cost guides.

Semrush generated 47 ideas: 23 content, 8 semantic, 8 SERP-feature and 8 backlink ideas, with no technical ideas for these pages.

The Extensions page is a high/medium priority example. Semrush recommends:

- include a primary phrase naturally in the title and H1;
- cover `house extensions london` and `house extensions west london` in the body where accurate;
- improve readability and add genuinely useful depth;
- discuss related concepts such as kitchen extensions, terraced houses and design-and-build;
- add aggregate rating markup only if it accurately represents visible, eligible reviews.

Do not blindly follow Semrush backlink suggestions. The raw lists contain many weak or spam-like domains. Manually vet prospects; credible candidates observed include FMB, Ideal Home, Living In magazines and relevant local community publications.

## Priority plan

1. Repair LocalBusiness schema and overlong titles.
2. Strengthen Extensions, Loft Conversions and Kitchens pages using the focused terms and real project evidence.
3. Let the newly updated Roofing page be crawled and tracked; add real roofing photos/case details when available.
4. Build a small number of legitimate local/trade links rather than buying directory packages.
5. Review Search Console impressions, clicks and indexed pages every two weeks; judge progress over 8–12 weeks, not day to day.

## Success measures

- important service pages begin receiving impressions independently of the homepage;
- tracked terms move into the top 50, then top 20;
- non-brand organic enquiries appear in the quote form and phone-call records;
- referring domains grow through credible local and trade sources;
- structured data errors reach zero on the next crawl.
